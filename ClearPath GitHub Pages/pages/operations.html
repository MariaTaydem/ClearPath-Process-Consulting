Case Study · Regulated Industry

Automated Document Management System

Life Sciences Laboratory · ISO Compliance & Accreditation Preparation · 2025–2026

A regulated laboratory preparing for ISO compliance and accreditation had 275 controlled documents with no consistent lifecycle management. Documents moved between drafts, review, revision, approval, and archiving through manual processes that were fragile and impossible to audit reliably.

I designed and built a complete Electronic Document Management System using SharePoint as the document repository and Power Automate as the automation engine — covering the full controlled document lifecycle from creation through archiving.

  • Designed 14 automated and scheduled Power Automate flows — document creation, SOP review cycles, revision routing, approval workflows, and quarterly integrity checks
  • Built a staged approval pattern using a SharePoint staging list to eliminate document-locking failures during user file moves
  • Implemented automated year-based CD numbering, modulo-based SOP distribution across review months, and escalation digest emails to prevent bottlenecks
  • Authored comprehensive user manuals and updated the organization's Quality Service Manual to reflect the new automated workflows
  • Produced full system documentation — all flows, metadata columns, design patterns, and SharePoint architecture — enabling any future administrator to maintain the system independently
Outcome

275 controlled documents under full lifecycle management · 14 automated flows · ISO-compliant · Accreditation-ready · Complete documentation suite including user manuals and updated Quality Service Manual

Tools: SharePoint · Power Automate · Office Scripts · Microsoft Teams Approvals · Excel

Case Study · Healthcare Services

Operations Overhaul — Healthcare Services Practice

Small Business · Compliance, Growth & Efficiency · Ongoing

A growing occupational therapy practice was operating with disorganized file systems, manual contractor tracking, and unclear administrative workflows. The owner was spending significant time on processes that should have been running automatically.

  • Designed and implemented a structured file system bringing order to contracts, clinical records, and administrative documents
  • Migrated all client and business records to a compliant cloud platform
  • Built contractor timesheet templates linked to a master summary dashboard — giving the owner real-time visibility into labor costs without manual consolidation
  • Designed a workers' compensation session tracker with automated threshold alerts flagging when to submit requests for additional authorized sessions
  • Developed SOPs for recurring administrative processes and supported physical space reorganization for operational clarity and efficiency
Outcome

Owner reclaimed hours previously lost to manual tracking and document searches · Contractor management running on automated dashboards · SOPs in place for key recurring processes

Tools: Excel (linked sheets, conditional formatting) · Compliant cloud platform · SOP templates
