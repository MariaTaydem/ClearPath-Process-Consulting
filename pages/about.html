About

Maria Taydem, Ph.D., PMP

Process consultant, project manager, chemist-turned-systems-thinker. Based in Knoxville, TN.

I have always been fascinated by how things work — and how they can work better. I started as a chemist, studying catalysts: tiny change agents that make processes faster, easier, cleaner. I loved the thinking, but the lab was a bit lonely. I wanted to work with people.

So I switched my focus to a larger scale, same goal. I've spent years helping organizations — federal programs, research universities, community colleges, and small businesses — untangle the systems that were slowing them down. The problems are always recognizable: things that worked fine when you were smaller, now costing time and energy you don't have. Processes that live in someone's head instead of somewhere findable. Growth that has outpaced the systems underneath it.

My background spans federal program management (three concurrent DOE-funded programs totaling $15M), higher education leadership (Dean of Mathematics and Sciences, Director of Project Management at a major research university), and small business consulting. My Ph.D. in chemistry means I'm equally comfortable in regulated, technical, and scientific environments — whether that's a lab preparing for ISO accreditation or a research-adjacent organization navigating compliance.

I come in, figure out where things are breaking, and build something that fits your business and actually sticks. I don't hand you a template and wish you luck. I stay until it works.

When I'm not untangling someone's systems, you'll find me in the garden, fly fishing, or tending my fiber arts projects. I rely on a daily meditation practice to quiet my mind — which, it turns out, is also pretty good preparation for walking into a chaotic organization and seeing it clearly.
