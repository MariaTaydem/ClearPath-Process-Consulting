Case Study · Higher Education

PM Infrastructure Build — Digital Learning Unit

University Setting · Rapid Growth Environment · 2024–2025

A digital learning unit was expanding rapidly with no structured project management infrastructure in place. Five internal teams and 20+ academic partners were coordinating through fragmented spreadsheets and ad-hoc communication, with no unified visibility into launch timelines or partner status.

I built the unit's first structured PM infrastructure from scratch — designing frameworks, establishing norms, coordinating with external vendors, and stepping into interim leadership roles as organizational need arose.

  • Designed Asana-based tracking frameworks with automations and cross-functional task management across five internal teams and 20+ academic partners
  • Directed weekly cross-functional stand-ups that grew from 6 to 15 members — establishing shared workflows, communication norms, and escalation paths
  • Coordinated with external vendors to ensure alignment with internal timelines and launch requirements
  • Stepped into interim Partnership Manager and Program Launch Manager roles when organizational need arose
  • Cut application data verification cycle time from 10 days to under 4 through Agile-inspired workflow redesign — a 60% efficiency gain
  • Consolidated fragmented partner tracking into single-source dashboards — keeps everyone informed and confident about partner health, enrollment trends, and launch readiness
Outcome

11 program launches delivered · 40% enrollment increase supported · 60% reduction in data verification cycle time · Full PM infrastructure including tracking, reporting, partner onboarding, and launch protocols

Tools: Asana · Flowsana · SharePoint · Excel dashboards · Agile methods

Case Study · Federal Programs

Federal Program Portfolio — Three Concurrent Programs

Federal Contractor · $15M Portfolio · 2021–2024

Managed three concurrent federally funded talent development programs totaling $15M — each with distinct participant populations, compliance structures, pay schedules, and sponsor reporting requirements. Served as primary liaison for 40+ federal agency offices while managing full program operations independently.

  • Independently sequenced priorities across three programs on parallel timelines — ensuring compliance, on-time deliverables, and sponsor satisfaction simultaneously
  • Managed full HR operations for approximately 300 program participants — onboarding, offboarding, payroll coordination, benefits, housing allowances, and travel reimbursement
  • Designed and delivered a 40-hour onboarding and professional development program for 60 participants annually across 40+ sponsoring offices
  • Sustained multi-year sponsor renewals through proactive relationship management, strategic review sessions, and outcome-based reporting
  • Supervised one program specialist per program while maintaining accountability for accuracy and compliance across all three concurrent populations
Outcome

$15M portfolio managed across 3 concurrent programs · 300 participants · 40+ federal sponsors · Multi-year renewals secured · Company recognition award for collaboration quality

Tools: Excel (macros, dashboards) · Agile methods · Federal compliance frameworks
