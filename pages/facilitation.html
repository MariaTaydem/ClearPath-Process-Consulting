Facilitation & Training

Learning that actually changes how people work.

Workshop design, active learning facilitation, and professional development programs for organizations navigating change, growth, or new systems.

The approach

Participants don't just hear about it — they experience it.

Every workshop I design uses active learning principles throughout. Participants work through real problems, discuss with peers, surface their own expertise, and leave with something they can actually use — not just a slide deck to forget about later.

I've designed and facilitated professional development for approximately 350 faculty and staff in higher education, a 40-hour onboarding program for 60 federal interns annually, and workshops for K-12 leadership, corporate staff, and community organizations. The context changes; the commitment to genuine engagement doesn't.

Sample Work · Active Learning

Active Learning Faculty Development Initiative

University Client · Full-Initiative Proposal · 2025

Designed a multi-phase faculty development initiative introducing, demonstrating, and sustaining active learning practices across half-day, full-day, and multi-session formats.

  • Half-day and full-day workshop formats — participants experience 7 to 11 active learning techniques firsthand
  • Techniques include Think-Pair-Share, Jigsaw, Case Study, Gallery Walk, Socratic Method, Problem-Based Learning, Concept Mapping, and more
  • Community of Practice model — self-selected cohorts meet monthly, share observations, and support each other through course redesign
  • Curated resource hub built into the institution's existing faculty development infrastructure
  • Classroom observation and feedback sessions available for deeper individual support
Sample deliverable

Full proposal available including half-day and full-day workshop agendas, community of practice model, resource hub plan, and investment structure.

Case Study · Network Building

Professional Networks & Communities of Practice

Higher Education · Multi-Institution · 2018–2021

Built three regional professional networks and learning communities from scratch — because infrastructure for shared learning multiplies what any single leader or organization can accomplish alone.

  • Founded the Developmental Education Fellows Program connecting eight Illinois community colleges
  • Founded the Faculty Development Consortium of Northern Illinois — cross-institutional network of 9 institutions
  • Founded the Deans of Northern Illinois network — monthly peer learning community of 40+ community college deans
  • Served as President, Chicago Area Faculty Development Network
Outcome

Three self-sustaining networks built from nothing · 40+ members across communities · Multi-institution learning infrastructure that outlasted any single project or role
